# Avalon Initiative

Building **Avalon Protocol** — an open, Rust-based interoperability protocol for
persistent cross-game player identity: identity, friends, guilds,
achievements/attestations, and opt-in permissioned social infrastructure that
lets independent games and apps recognize the same players and communities
without surrendering control of their own worlds.

This org is being staged ahead of migrating the protocol, hub, and supporting
crates out of their original single-repo prototype into their own repositories
here.
